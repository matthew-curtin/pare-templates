The three food photographs were chosen **before** the three dishes on
the home page were, not after. One of them is why the menu says red
mullet rather than plaice: the picture shows whole round fish over
coals, plaice is a flatfish, and changing one word in the content was
the honest fix. Captioning that photograph "plaice" would not have been.

`fire.jpg` and `produce.jpg` are re-encoded a little harder than the
rest — they are detailed, they compress badly, and at their original
quality the two of them alone came to most of the image budget.

There are no photographs of people. Every face on this site is a
monogram, because a real face attached to an invented person sits badly.

## Type

**Instrument Serif** for display and **Karla** for everything else, both
through `next/font`, which downloads and self-hosts them at build time.
Nothing is fetched from a font CDN at runtime, so there is no
third-party request, no flash of a fallback face, and the site works
offline.

Instrument Serif is a high-contrast display face that holds up at the
size the hero uses it and would be unreadable at body size, which is
why it never appears below a heading. Karla is a warm grotesque with
slightly odd letterforms — enough character for a small restaurant, dull
enough to read a menu in.

## Colour

Original to this template. A warm, smoky near-black with an ember accent
and a cool herb green.

The base is deliberately brown rather than blue: the fleet already has a
cool dark site, and a wood-fired restaurant that reads cold is the wrong
room. Ink is bone rather than white, because pure white on warm black
glares.

## Everything else

The Coppice wordmark is set in the display face rather than drawn. There
are no third-party assets, no icon fonts and no illustration libraries.
