Products are cropped square at 1000px because that is the shape the
cards and the product page use; the rest are cropped to the ratio of
the surface they sit on. 1.1 MB for the set of eleven.

Each alt text describes the photograph that is actually there. Worth
saying, because they were written before the pictures were chosen and
three had to be corrected afterwards — a "metal scoop" that turned out
to be plastic, and two that overstated what was in frame.

The AeroPress photograph was swapped during review for one that does
not lead with the wordmark. Naming the device is unavoidable in a
recipe about it; making its logo the focal point of the page is a
choice, and not one a template should make on its user's behalf.

## Type

[Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk) by
Florian Karsten for headings and prices, and
[Instrument Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans) by
Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jordan Egstad for body and interface text. Both
are served through `next/font`, which downloads them at build time and
self-hosts them — so the finished shop makes no request to Google.

## Everything else

The wordmark, the roast scale, the flavour chart, the basket icon and
the brew timer are all drawn in CSS or inline SVG inside this template.
There are no other third-party assets.
