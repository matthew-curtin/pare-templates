**The retry schedule is now seven attempts over 25 hours**, up from five
over 90 minutes.

| Attempt | Was | Now |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | immediately | immediately |
| 2 | 30s | 30s |
| 3 | 5m | 2m 30s |
| 4 | 30m | 12m 30s |
| 5 | 90m | 1h 12m |
| 6 | — | 7h 12m |
| 7 | — | 25h 12m |

The old schedule gave up inside two hours, which is shorter than a great
many deploys and most weekend outages. Roughly one exhausted delivery in
five under the old schedule would have succeeded under this one.

**What this changes for you.** A delivery can now stay `pending` for over
a day, so anything that treated "not succeeded after two hours" as
terminal needs its window widening. Subscribe to
`rookery.delivery.exhausted` rather than inferring failure from elapsed
time.

**Jitter.** Each interval varies by up to 10%, so a recovering subscriber
no longer receives every queued delivery in the same instant.

**Deprecated:** the `max_attempts` field on endpoint creation. It is
ignored, and returns a warning header. It will be removed in a future
version.
